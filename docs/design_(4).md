# Design rules (every Bolt project)

- The site must not feel cookie-cutter or AI-generated. No generic purple
  gradients, no default centered-hero layouts.
- Before writing UI, consult the per-project design references supplied in the
  brief. Never paste an entire inspiration library; use only what fits this
  build.
- Typography and color: commit to a real pairing with a clear hierarchy. Body
  text must be readable at a glance.
- Motion: small, purposeful details over big animations. Nothing that blocks
  content or slows the page.
- Every page must look intentional at desktop and at 390px mobile width.
